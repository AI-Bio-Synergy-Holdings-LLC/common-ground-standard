Institutional Alignment Brief

Bring your institution into a review process built to earn trust.

Common Ground Standard is developing a structured route for institutions that can contribute public-interest review, affected-party governance, evidence, or implementation knowledge. This route is being defined before any accreditation, certification, or adoption claim is offered.

Check organizational fit Read the participation boundary

02 — Why this phase exists

Institutional depth should be built before institutional authority is claimed.

The current public-review site makes it possible for individuals and organizations to test one claim and propose one focused change. The institutional pathway adds a second layer: recurring review capacity, defined representation, evidence requirements, and implementation tests.

This phase is intended to learn what a credible future governance body would need. Participation creates a documented contribution record. It does not create membership, accreditation, or authority to speak for affected communities.

03 — Contribution roles

Four ways an institution can strengthen the review.

Lived-experience and affected-party review

Tests the draft against the rights, resources, work, learning, and trust of people directly affected.

Domain and evidence review

Tests claims, thresholds, metrics, and evidence requirements using relevant research or professional knowledge.

Implementation and feasibility review

Tests whether safeguards can work in real institutions, infrastructure projects, and public programs.

Independent assurance and anti-capture review

Tests conflicts, reviewer independence, auditability, and the ability to stop unsupported public claims.

05 — Institutional pathway

Five stages from interest to a documented review role.

  1. 1

    Orient

    Choose a review domain and understand the participation boundary.

  2. 2

    Disclose

    Identify mission, expertise, material conflicts, and requested role.

  3. 3

    Contribute

    Submit one grounded critique, evidence requirement, or implementation test.

  4. 4

    Record

    Publish the role, boundaries, status, and accepted changes.

  5. 5

    Advance

    Become eligible for future recognition after independent governance exists.

06 — Eligibility

Fit is determined by contribution and boundaries, not prestige.

Good fit

  • Shares a documented public-interest objective
  • Can name the constituency or domain it represents
  • Accepts public conflict disclosure and record requirements
  • Accepts correction and public-record requirements

Not eligible for a formal role

  • Seeks endorsement, a badge, or marketing legitimacy before review
  • Requires confidential influence over public safeguards
  • Cannot disclose material conflicts or funding relationships
  • Treats participation as authority to speak for affected communities

08 — Operating controls

Know how a live submission would be handled before sharing information.

This controlled staging verification transmits only authorized synthetic data. Live intake remains disabled. If live intake is separately authorized, the following controls apply to every institutional review note.

Privacy + access

Private by default.

Formspree will process the submitted fields for delivery. Access is limited to the interim intake owner and specifically assigned reviewers. Public listing requires separate written permission. Do not submit sensitive, confidential, privileged, or restricted information.

Retention

Time-limited records.

Incomplete or declined notes are retained for no more than twelve months unless a legal, security, correction, or dispute reason requires longer retention. Active contribution records may be retained through the work and for up to twenty-four months after closure. Retention is reviewed at least quarterly.

Decision + recourse

Reasons, correction, and reconsideration.

A note may be corrected before final disposition or withdrawn at any time. A declined or restricted decision may be reconsidered within thirty calendar days. Requests awaiting clarification may be marked inactive after thirty calendar days without a response.

Conflict + removal

Pause when independence is uncertain.

A material steward conflict pauses the fit decision until an unconflicted external reviewer is assigned and recorded. Unmanageable conflicts, boundary violations, or misleading partnership, endorsement, accreditation, or certification claims may result in decline, pause, correction, or removal.

Request correction, withdrawal, reconsideration, deletion review, or conflict escalation at managingfounder@common-ground-standard.org. Requests are completed where consistent with legal, security, audit, and public-record obligations.
